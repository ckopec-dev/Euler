The correct answer to Project Euler Problem 418 is **7**.

This makes sense because:
- For n = 10^9 = 2^9 × 5^9
- The product of all divisors = (10^9)^(d(10^9)/2) where d(10^9) = (9+1)(9+1) = 100
- So the product = (10^9)^50 = 10^450 = (2×5)^450 = 2^450 × 5^450
- The distinct prime factors are just {2, 5}
- Therefore, sum = 2 + 5 = 7
