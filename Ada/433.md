## Problem Statement
The problem asks us to find the sum of all integers k from 1 to 10^12 such that the number of divisors of k is divisible by 3.

## Analysis

Key insights:
- For a number n with prime factorization n = p₁^a₁ × p₂^a₂ × ... × pₖ^aₖ, the number of divisors is (a₁+1)(a₂+1)...(aₖ+1)
- We want this product to be divisible by 3
- This happens when at least one of the (aᵢ+1) terms is divisible by 3, i.e., when some aᵢ ≡ 2 (mod 3)
