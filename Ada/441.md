I need to solve Euler Problem 441, which asks for the sum of the reciprocals of the squares of all positive integers that are divisible by at least one prime p where p ≥ 10.

Let me first understand what this problem is asking:
- We want the sum: Σ(1/n²) for all n such that n is divisible by at least one prime p ≥ 10
- This is equivalent to finding the sum over all positive integers n that have at least one prime factor ≥ 10

## Approach

I'll use the inclusion-exclusion principle:
1. Calculate the sum of 1/n² for all positive integers n (which is π²/6)
2. Subtract the sum of 1/n² for integers n that are only divisible by primes < 10
3. The primes less than 10 are: 2, 3, 5, 7

So: Result = π²/6 - (sum over n with only prime factors 2,3,5,7)
