# Euler Problem 447 Solution in Ada

## Problem Statement
Find the sum of all distinct values of f(n) for n ≤ 10^8, where f(n) is the largest integer k such that k! divides n!.

## Analysis
This problem requires finding the largest k such that k! divides n!. This is equivalent to finding the largest k such that for each prime p, the power of p in k! is less than or equal to the power of p in n!.

The power of prime p in n! is given by Legendre's formula: ∑_{i=1}^{∞} ⌊n/p^i⌋

## Solution Approach
1. For each n from 1 to 10^8, compute f(n)
2. Keep track of distinct values of f(n)
3. Sum all distinct values

However, we can optimize this significantly:
- f(n) = max{k : k! | n!}
- This is equivalent to finding the largest k such that for all primes p, v_p(k!) ≤ v_p(n!)
- Where v_p(m) is the power of prime p in m!
