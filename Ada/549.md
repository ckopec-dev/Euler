# Euler Problem 549 Solution in Ada

Euler Problem 549 asks: "Find the smallest positive integer n such that n! is divisible by 10^18."

Let me solve this step by step.

## Analysis

For n! to be divisible by 10^18 = 2^18 × 5^18, we need:
- At least 18 factors of 2 in n!
- At least 18 factors of 5 in n!

Since there are always more factors of 2 than factors of 5 in n!, we only need to ensure that n! has at least 18 factors of 5.

The number of factors of 5 in n! is given by:
⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + ⌊n/125⌋ + ⌊n/625⌋ + ...
